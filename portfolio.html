Real Estate

One Brand.
Two Digital Systems.

Logic Realty — Hermanus & Overberg
The Problem

A growing real estate agency needed more than a brochure site. They needed a marketing platform that generated leads and a live rental listings system their agents could manage daily — without paying ongoing licensing fees for software that didn't fit their workflow.

We built two interconnected systems: a full marketing website with lead capture and a free seller's guide funnel, alongside a custom PHP and MySQL rental listings platform with agent login, live property management, and dynamically served listing pages. Both live under the same brand. Neither required a monthly SaaS subscription. The property platform currently manages live rental stock across Hermanus and the Overberg with zero third-party dependency.

Art & Gin — Premium SA Gin Brand
The Problem

Art & Gin is built around a story — an artisanal gin whose label carries original artwork by celebrated South African artist Portchie. The product needed a digital home that honoured that story without drowning it in a generic e-commerce template.

We built the site around the brand narrative, not the product catalogue. The artwork leads. The gin's origin, the artist's hand, the craft of distillation — these are the architecture of the experience, not an afterthought buried in an About page. Order functionality is present and seamless, but it exists in service of the story. The site makes you want the product before it even asks you to buy it.

VUE Architecture & Associates
The Problem

An architecture firm whose work includes cutting-edge 3D visualisation and bespoke residential design cannot have a website that looks like it came from a template library. Their online presence had to be as considered as their buildings.

We built a portfolio-first site where the work leads. 3D renders and project photography are treated with editorial weight — not shoved into a gallery grid. Services are clear. The contact path is direct. The result is a site that communicates the firm's design sensibility before a word is read, which is exactly how a design firm should be judged by a prospective client.
